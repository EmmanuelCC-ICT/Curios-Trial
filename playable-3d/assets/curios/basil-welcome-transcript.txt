Hi, I'm Basil. I said Tania couldn't easily turn Career Empire into something Curios teachers could make their own. Well, here we are!

Her prompts were simple: keep the world, avatars, buildings and characters; remove the careers content; and leave the original untouched. The first explorable copy took about fifteen minutes. The teacher tools, branding, app rooms and testing came afterwards.

Now the campus is a shared front door for teacher-created apps. Follow a sign and walk inside to open Archon Engine, Art Quest or Loopy Tunes. Return to Curios brings you back. Avatar Studio is still here.

Teachers can change names, conversations and room links in Teacher setup. Preview your ideas, then give ICT the settings to publish. Your curriculum gives this world its purpose.

Reuse. Repurpose. Recycle. Curios: learning without limits. Apparently, you can teach an old N P C new tricks.
